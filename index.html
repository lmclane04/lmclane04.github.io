I'm a senior at Stanford studying electrical engineering. I'm interested in the intersection of AI and hardware - both how hardware can better serve AI systems and how AI can accelerate hardware design cycles. Most of my experience is in digital design and computer architecture, but I also like getting closer to the physical side with analog and board-level design. I'm also interested in reinforcement learning and self-improving systems that learn to do things as well or better than humans.

I'm currently working on building self-improving agents for hardware design and verification in Tambe Lab. Before that, I worked on AI agents for cybersecurity and in Stanford AI Lab and data-driven dynamical system identification research in the Stanord Brains in Silicon Lab.

Outside of engineering, I'm passionate about philosophy and history. I've been learning about Greek and Roman history and philosophy as of late. I also like to play tennis and hike in my free time.
